# Code of Conduct

Gulliver follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language, or publishing someone's private information.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, open a short issue that only says you want to report a conduct problem (no
details), and the maintainer will contact you privately. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, issues or pull requests, and may block people who do not follow this code.
